My Journey

8:00 PM — HARVEST MOON COCOA

Waking Up in the Haunted Nursery

When the harvest moon rises above the crooked rooftops and the first owl hoots, I wake up with a sleepy yawn and polish my glowing amber eyes. Every Halloween evening, I wind up my phantom music box before tiptoeing to the kitchen for a warm mug of marshmallow hot chocolate, followed by a cozy bubble bath from mama!

10:15 PM — THE CANDLELIT WORKSHOP

Bottling Pumpkin-Spice Dreams

Dressed in my starry nightcap, I float down to my candlelit workshop and line up my glass dream jars. I fill each jar with golden starlight, gentle lullaby notes, and cinnamon-sweet wishes so not a single little ghost or bat has a scary dream tonight.

11:59 PM — CHIMNEY TELEPORTATION

Midnight Rooftop Deliveries

Once every jar is glowing bright, I hop onto my cloud cruiser and teleport from chimney to chimney across the sleeping neighborhood. After the last Halloween dream is safely delivered, I drift back home at dawn and curl up for my morning nap!
